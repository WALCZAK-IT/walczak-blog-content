When generating images for this blog follow the below conventions.

# Rules
* don't put the articles title in the image
* the image may be an infographic summarizing the article

# Core Visual Direction
Style: High-end architectural minimalism, tactile industrial realism, cinematic chiaroscuro, precision engineering.
Atmosphere: Dark, deliberate, calm, authoritative. Looks like a bespoke conceptual still from a high-budget architectural or industrial design documentary, not generic tech stock.
Aspect Ratio: 16:10 (native to the site’s card layout) or 16:9.
Composition: Strong focal point, generous negative dark space, balanced geometric composition, clean silhouettes.

# Color Palette & Lighting Rules
Background & Dominant Tones: Pitch black, obsidian, matte charcoal, deep slate (#050605, #000000).
Primary Accent Light: Warm copper, burnt amber, brushed brass rim light (#D98A4A).
Subtle Secondary Tones: Muted forest green, dark moss, desaturated pine (#4F6353).
Highlights: Warm paper off-white (#F3F1EA), frosted glass refractions, sharp specular edge highlights.
Lighting: Low-key lighting, dramatic directional soft box, tactile rim lighting carving silhouettes out of deep shadows.
